Before You Order

Delivery, Returns and Support

The commitments every order on APEX MARKET is held to, published in full rather than summarised into a slogan.

Delivery Times
Lagos and Abuja within 48 hours. Other state capitals in 2 to 4 working days. Addresses outside the capitals in 3 to 6 working days.
Delivery Cost
A flat ₦2,500 anywhere in Nigeria, and free on orders above ₦100,000.
Returns Window
14 days from delivery on unused items in their original packaging. Faulty or misdescribed items are collected at our cost.
Refunds
Processed within 5 working days of the returned item reaching our Lagos warehouse, to the account the order was paid from.
Payment Methods
Verified card, bank transfer and USSD. Payment on delivery is available in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt.
Seller Verification
Every seller provides CAC registration and a valid photo ID before listing, and each listing is reviewed by a person before it goes live.
Support Hours
Monday to Saturday, 8:00 AM to 7:00 PM (WAT), by phone, email or WhatsApp. Messages sent outside those hours are answered the next working morning.
