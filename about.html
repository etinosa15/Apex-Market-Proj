About APEX MARKET

Quality You Can Trust, Delivered to Your Door

We started APEX MARKET to make honest, verified shopping accessible to every Nigerian household, one reliable delivery at a time.

Our Story

Built in Lagos, Trusted Nationwide

APEX MARKET launched in 2019 out of a single warehouse in Lagos with one goal: to close the trust gap between Nigerian shoppers and online sellers. Too many buyers had been burned by unclear listings and unreliable delivery, so we set out to be the marketplace that keeps its word.

Since then we have grown into a nationwide operation connecting verified sellers with shoppers in all 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory, without losing the close attention to quality that we started with. Every listing is reviewed, every seller is vetted, and every order is tracked from checkout to doorstep.

Today, APEX MARKET is where Nigerians go when they want to shop with confidence: quality products, delivered on time, on terms that are written down and published.
