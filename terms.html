1. About these terms

APEX MARKET operates as a marketplace. For most items the contract of sale is between you and the seller, and we act as the platform, the payment collector and the party that handles delivery and returns. Where a listing is marked as sold by APEX MARKET, we are the seller.

2. Your account

You must be 18 or over to hold an account. You are responsible for keeping your password private and for orders placed from your account. Tell us at once if you think somebody else has access to it.

We may suspend an account that is used for fraud, for chargeback abuse, or to harass our staff or sellers. Where we suspend an account we will tell you why, and any money owed to you will still be paid.

3. Orders

A listing is an invitation to buy, not an offer. Your order becomes a contract only when we send the confirmation email. If an item turns out to be out of stock after you order, or if a price was listed in obvious error, we may cancel and will refund you in full within 5 working days.

You can cancel an order at any point before it is dispatched by replying to your confirmation email or by contacting support.

4. Prices and payment

  • All prices are in Nigerian naira and include VAT where it applies.
  • We accept verified cards, bank transfer and USSD. Payment on delivery is available in Lagos, Abuja and Port Harcourt.
  • Card details are handled by our payment processor and are not stored on our systems. See the Privacy Policy.
  • Delivery is charged at a flat ₦2,500 anywhere in Nigeria, and is free on orders above ₦100,000.

5. Delivery

We deliver to all 36 states and the Federal Capital Territory. Our published windows, counted in working days from dispatch, are:

  • Lagos and Abuja: within 48 hours.
  • Other state capitals: 2 to 4 working days.
  • Addresses outside the state capitals: 3 to 6 working days.

You will get a tracking reference when the order is dispatched. Someone aged 18 or over must be present to receive the parcel. If the courier cannot reach you after two attempts the parcel returns to our Lagos warehouse and we will contact you to arrange redelivery; a second delivery fee applies where the failed attempts were not our fault.

If an order misses its published window by more than three working days and the delay is ours, we refund the delivery fee without you having to ask.

6. Returns

You have 14 days from delivery to return an item. To be accepted it must be unused, in its original packaging and complete with any accessories. Start a return by replying to your confirmation email or by contacting support.

Return postage is yours to pay if you simply changed your mind. If the item is faulty, damaged in transit or not what the listing described, we collect it at our cost.

For reasons of hygiene and safety, the following cannot be returned unless faulty: underwear, earphones and in-ear audio, opened cosmetics and personal care items, perishable goods, and any item sold as a clearance or final-sale line and labelled as such on the listing page.

7. Refunds

Refunds are processed within 5 working days of the returned item reaching our Lagos warehouse and passing inspection, and are paid to the account the order was paid from. Your bank may take a further 3 to 10 working days to show the credit. Where an item fails inspection because it has been used or is incomplete, we will tell you within those 5 days and return it to you at our cost.

8. Listings and sellers

Every seller provides CAC registration and a valid photo ID before listing, and every listing is reviewed by a person before it goes live. Sellers are responsible for the accuracy of their descriptions, images and stock levels, and for holding any licence their category requires.

We remove listings that are counterfeit, unsafe, illegal to sell in Nigeria, or materially misdescribed, and we remove sellers who repeat those failures. If you have bought an item that turns out to be counterfeit, contact us: we refund in full, collect the item at our cost, and you keep no obligation to the seller.
