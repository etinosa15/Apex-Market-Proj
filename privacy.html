Privacy Policy

What We Collect, and Why

A plain account of the information APEX MARKET holds about you, what we do with it, who else sees it, and how to have it removed.

Last updated 1 September 2026

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy covers everyone who uses apexmarket.ng: shoppers with an account, shoppers who check out as guests, sellers listing products, and visitors who only browse. APEX MARKET Limited, 14 Marina Street, Lagos Island, Lagos, is the data controller responsible for the information described here.

We process personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Where this policy and that Act disagree, the Act governs.

2. Information we collect

We collect three kinds of information, and nothing beyond what the service needs to work.

Information you give us

  • Your name, email address and phone number when you create an account or place an order.
  • The delivery address, and any access notes you add for the courier.
  • Messages you send us through the contact form, by email or on WhatsApp.
  • For sellers: business name, CAC registration number, a valid photo ID and settlement bank details.

Information created by your orders

  • What you bought, when, at what price, and where it was delivered.
  • Returns, refunds and support tickets connected to those orders.

Information collected automatically

  • Your IP address, browser type and the pages you requested, written to our hosting provider's server logs.

3. What we use it for

Your information is used to take payment, get an order to the right address, answer you when you contact us, verify sellers, investigate fraud and chargebacks, and meet our tax and record-keeping obligations. We also use aggregate figures, with names removed, to understand which categories are worth stocking.

We send marketing email only to people who have asked for it. Every marketing email carries an unsubscribe link that works on the first click, and unsubscribing does not affect the messages we must send about an order you have placed.

4. Payment information

Card details are entered on the page of our payment processor and are never stored on APEX MARKET servers. We retain only the last four digits of the card and the authorisation reference, which is what our support desk needs to trace a payment or issue a refund.

5. Who else sees your information

We do not sell personal data. We share the minimum necessary with:

  • The seller of the item you bought, who receives your name, delivery address and phone number so the order can be packed and handed over.
  • Our delivery partners, who receive the same details in order to deliver and to call you if they cannot find the address.
  • Our payment processor, who receives the order amount and reference.
  • Law enforcement or a regulator, where we are required by law to disclose, and only to the extent required.

Each seller and courier is bound by a written agreement that limits their use of your details to fulfilling your order.

6. How long we keep it

Order and payment records are kept for six years, which is the retention period our tax and accounting obligations require. Support correspondence is kept for two years. Marketing consent records are kept for as long as the consent stands, plus one year. If you close your account, we delete or anonymise everything that falls outside those periods.

7. Cookies

These pages set no cookies at all. There is no account session to hold open, no cart to carry between pages and no analytics package running, so nothing is written to your browser and there is nothing here for you to refuse. We do not run advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings at any time; on this site it will change nothing. If we ever do set one, this section will be rewritten before it happens and the date at the top of the page will move with it.

8. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may:

  • Ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Have inaccurate details corrected.
  • Ask us to delete data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing at any time.
  • Object to a particular use of your data, or ask us to restrict it while a dispute is resolved.
  • Complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you believe we have got this wrong.

Write to privacy@apexmarket.ng and we will respond within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, so that we do not hand your data to somebody else.

9. Security

This site is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your browser and our host is encrypted in transit. Access to customer records is limited to staff who need it for their work, and that access is logged. If a breach occurs that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

10. Children

APEX MARKET is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

11. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes we update the date at the top of the page. If a change materially affects how we use your data, we will email account holders at least 14 days before it takes effect.
